## Owner actions required for Google discovery
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in to the Google account that will own the site.
2. Add URL-prefix property `https://codenetz.cc.cd/` (include HTTPS and trailing slash), or add a DNS domain property for `codenetz.cc.cd`.
3. For the URL-prefix property, download Google's exact HTML verification file or copy its HTML meta verification tag. Add that file to this repository root or that meta tag to the homepage head. **Do not invent a verification code.**
4. Complete ownership verification in Search Console, then open Sitemaps and submit `sitemap.xml`.
5. Use URL Inspection for homepage and priority service pages and monitor indexing, impressions, clicks, queries, and average position.
6. Optionally connect verified CodeNetz property to analytics/reporting software. The site currently has no confirmed Google Search Console connection.

## Local discovery
Google Business Profile may only be appropriate if the business has a genuine eligible in-person customer interaction or qualifying service-area operation. Do not list virtual offices, unstaffed locations or fictitious addresses in Aurangabad, Pune, New York, Dubai or other cities. Google may display "near me" results based heavily on local proximity.

## Content, credibility and conversions
- Publish verifiable past work, with permission and authentic results when available. Portfolio concept images should not be presented as verified case studies.
- Request genuine customer reviews from completed projects; never invent reviews or customers.
- Add publicly verifiable company identity and a legitimate business address *only after the owner confirms it*.
- Build relevant referrals from partner companies, industry directories, chamber memberships and credible local organizations.
- For true automatic lead capture rather than email-app/WhatsApp message preparation, connect a verified form handling service or back-end endpoint with spam protection, consent and secure storage.
- Use appropriate analytics and privacy disclosures if tracking tools are enabled.

## Monthly review
Review Google Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, search queries, indexed URLs and incoming enquiries. Improve pages with high impressions but weak CTR. Compare visitor-to-enquiry conversion, not just traffic.

**No SEO implementation can guarantee #1 rankings or automatic customers.** Search engines decide crawl, indexing and rankings; legitimate content and business proof build long-term confidence.
