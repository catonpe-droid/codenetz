Privacy Policy

Privacy matters to us.

A simple explanation of how we handle information shared with CodeNetz.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Information you choose to share

When you contact us, you may share your name, email address, phone number and project details. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and discuss possible services.

How the contact form works

Our website's contact form opens your email application with a prepared message. The form itself does not send or store your information on our website server. Your email provider handles the message when you choose to send it.

Third-party services

Our website may load fonts from an external font provider. If you choose to contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, those services may process your information under their own policies.

Cookies and analytics

At launch, this website does not use advertising cookies or an analytics tracking service. If this changes, this policy should be updated.

Keeping information

We may keep business enquiries and related communications as reasonably needed to respond, work on a project or maintain business records. You can contact us to ask about your information.

Your choices

Please do not send passwords, confidential financial records or other sensitive information through this public enquiry form. For privacy requests, email codenetztech@gmail.com.

Updates

We may update this policy as our website and services develop. The latest version will be published on this page.
